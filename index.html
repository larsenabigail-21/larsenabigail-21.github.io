About Me

Hi, my name is Abigail. I am an artist based in Colorado specializing in painting Much of my work is inspired my themes of women's empowerment, social justice, mental health, environmental consciousness, and my personal journey. I am currently pursuing my undergraduate degree, my ultimate goal is to earn a BFA in Art with an emphisis in painting and a graphic design certificate. I was recently accepted to westerns BFA program, where I will continue my journey and work on a series for my gallery my senior year.

Current Series

My current series is about my experience being mexican american. Asking myself; "What does it mean to me to be Chicana?" "How has being mixed shaped my life?" Each of these pieces tell some aspects of my culture, and my story of being mixed.
