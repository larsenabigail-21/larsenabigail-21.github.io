Art by Abby

In my current classes, I have been working on a variety of projects. Currently I have classes in painting and digital
design. In my painting class, I am working on my own series, about my experience being mixed-race and Chicana.
For this series, I am using artist Frida Kahlo for inspiration. These projects showcase my skills and creativity in both
digital and physical realms. I am excited to share them with you!
