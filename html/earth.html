The Earth and the Sky

TW- mentions of death
Memento Mori is a Latin phrase that means, remember, you must die. It is a symbolic trope acting as a reminder of inevitable death.
Death makes people uncomfortable, which, granted, is a hard conversation to have, but also a reality that we would rather ignore. Psychologist, Corinne Sweet, says, “People find death a difficult topic to discuss as it usually brings up a lot of feelings: anxiety, fear, awkwardness, sadness. We tend to pretend, as a culture, that it's not going to happen.”
I think that is why people are religious. I think, religion takes advantage of the fear people have about death, which is great if that is what you believe in and that works for you, faith is important, and sometimes means survival. When the topic that I don’t believe in life after death or God, arises with people who believe in religion, they are often put back, and they will say to me “I don’t understand how you can believe there is nothing, and that does not scare you.” Or "If there is nothing after you die then what is the point." That is the point, that... is exactly what gives life purpose, that you only get to do it once. Accept the impermanence of life, and then choose to live it anyway. I don't wish to live my life for the satisfaction and acceptance of a god, I would rather just live and do things I wish to do, just because I want them, and they make sense to me. I don't fear death, not because I want to embrace death, but rather because I have accepted that it is inevitable.
“Death must be so beautiful. To lie in the soft brown earth, with grasses waving above one’s head, and listen to silence. To have no yesterday, and no tomorrow. To forget time, to forget life. To be at peace. You can help me. You can open for me the portals of death's house: for love is always with you, and love is stronger than death is.” - Oscar Wilde. This whole piece is based around this quote. Of course, death will be often inconsolable, and a gut wrenching battle, but just because someone dies doesn’t mean that love will vanish.
Energy cannot be created or destroyed. The law of conservation of energy. That is all we are, as people, energy. The blood that is in all our veins contains hemoglobin, a protein that has four iron atoms in its structure. Iron is naturally produced in one place; it is only forged at the core of dying stars. Supernova explosions result when the cores of massive stars have exhausted their fuel supplies and burned everything into iron and nickel. Your body is built and kept alive from pieces of stardust. With that being said, I believe while our body dies our energy must go somewhere, even if it is not the afterlife. I imagine my body under the ground surrounded by the earth, while my soul is in the sky next to the stars, and that doesn’t sound scary at all.
